        HOW TO PLAY

        Walk: arrow keys or W A S D. Run: hold Shift. Use or read: E, Space or Enter. Minimap size: M or click it. Menu: Esc. Fullscreen: F. On a touch screen: the arrow pad and the E button.

        Punch in at the time clock by the lockers. Shop at the Dock: take stock, carry it, and put it down with Q. T opens your tray, N your notebook. Use the lift or the stairs to change floors.
