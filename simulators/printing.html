How this model works

Every number comes from the pattern's geometry. Faces that hang past the support angle get pillars underneath. The layer count follows from the height. Each layer's cross-section area sets the peel load. Stair-stepping gives the surface roughness on slanted faces. Cure depth comes from exposure through a standard working curve. The rules for burnout, trapped resin and shrinkage are simplified from general practice, not from your resin's data sheet.

    Layer area through the print

    Each layer is peeled off the bottom of the tank. A large layer needs a hard pull, and a sudden jump in area is worse. Tilting the part spreads one big layer into many small ones.
