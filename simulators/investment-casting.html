    A teaching model, not validated foundry software. The investment, burnout and freezing relations are simple trend models with typical values for a gypsum-bonded investment, not your supplier's data sheet. The alloy properties are typical, the oven lag is a lumped estimate and the defect probabilities are tuned for instruction. Check temperatures and times against the investment manufacturer's instructions and with a test flask.
