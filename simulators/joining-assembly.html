Joining and assembly labBeta v0.1

The last step in the life of the part: how do you join the pieces, and will the assembly still fit and hold? Weigh bolting against welding and the other ways to join, tighten a bolt into cast aluminum, weld 6061-T6 and watch it soften, then stack the tolerances of the parts to see how many assemblies fit.

This is a beta. If something breaks or is confusing, send feedback.

Tensile test across the weld

The strain concentrates in the softened zone next to the weld, so the joint stretches much less than the plate. The specimen is drawn stretched more than real.

Results

    A teaching model, not validated design software. The joint choice is engineering judgement, written out so you can change it. The bolted-joint relations are textbook (torque-tension, joint stiffness, thread stripping, fatigue) with typical constants; the weld heat-affected zone uses a thin-plate heat-flow estimate, and the strength, distortion and defect rules are simple trend models; the stack-up uses typical process spreads. Real preload, weld quality and part spread depend on the shop. Check any number you rely on against codes, data sheets and test pieces.
