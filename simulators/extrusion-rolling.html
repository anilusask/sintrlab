Extrusion and rolling labBeta v0.1

Push a hot billet through a die or roll a plate down, and see what the working does to the grains and to the strength along and across the part. Then anneal it. Three tensile tests show the difference, which explains why a bend line along the grain cracks first.

Results

    Strength and ductility map

    A teaching model, not validated process software. Hot flow stress follows a sinh law fitted to typical values, the ram force uses Johnson-type relations with a friction term, and the grain, texture, seam, coarse-ring and annealing rules are simple trend models. Real extrusion and rolling depend on die design, alloy chemistry and press history. Check any number you rely on against data and test pieces.
