Machining lab: turningBeta v0.1

Turn a 20 mm shaft section on a lathe. Pick the cutting speed, feed and depth, and follow the calculation through spindle speed, time, force, power and tool life to the number that matters: the surface roughness, on the first part and on the last part of the batch.

This is a beta. If something breaks or is confusing, send feedback.

The calculation

Working for a 20 mm bar with 4 mm of radius to remove in passes of equal depth, 42 mm of cut length, one feed for all passes. Red and amber rows are the ones that need attention.

Roughness against feed

Slow cutting builds up an edge that tears the surface. The grey zone is beyond what the spindle can do.

The batch

Result

    A teaching model, not validated process software. The formulas are textbook-level (Taylor tool life, specific cutting energy, the feed-and-nose-radius roughness formula) with typical constants, not your tools' data. The built-up edge, chatter and wear effects are simple trend models. Check the numbers against a handbook or your tooling supplier before relying on them.
