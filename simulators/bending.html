Forming lab: bendingBeta v0.1

Bend a strip and look inside it: the grains stretch on the outside and squash on the inside, the metal work hardens, and a tight enough bend cracks. Then bend a hook again and again and watch the ductility run out. Three tensile tests show what the bending did to the strength.

This is a beta. If something breaks or is confusing, send feedback.

    A teaching model, not validated process software. Strain hardening, springback and the cracking limit use textbook relations with typical constants; the strength of a strip cut from a bend, the fatigue life, the anneal grain sizes and the grain pictures are simple trend models. Real results depend on temper, grain direction, tooling and edge condition. Check the numbers against data and test pieces.
